About the data

Incident reporting comes from multiple independent security publishers and CISA advisories. Publishers choose what they report; coverage is not exhaustive and publication may lag the event.

Runtime, loss, target, and effects are shown only when the source text states them. Missing information is labeled “Not reported”; YORU will not guess or fill gaps with estimates.

Device matching is a keyword relevance check—not version-aware vulnerability analysis, a security scan, or a compromise diagnosis. Verify affected versions and fixes with the linked vendor/CISA advisory.
